About FeedSpace

FeedSpace is a community-driven social platform designed for students and young professionals in the Philippines. It's a space where you can share your thoughts, join communities that match your interests, and stay connected with the people who matter most to you.

We believe in fostering genuine connections, meaningful conversations, and a positive online environment. FeedSpace is built with simplicity and community at its core — no algorithms hiding your posts, no ads cluttering your feed, just real people connecting authentically.

Whether you're sharing a photo from your weekend adventure, posting about your latest project, or just looking for a community of like-minded individuals — FeedSpace is your home.

About Developers

FeedSpace was built by a passionate team of student developers from the Philippines who wanted to create a social platform that truly understands their community. The project started as a school initiative and grew into something much bigger.

We are committed to continuously improving the platform and listening to our users. If you have suggestions, feedback, or just want to say hi, feel free to reach out through our Help page.
