Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a post?
Click the "Create Post" button on the home feed or tap the + floating button at the bottom right. Write your message, attach photos if you like, and hit post!
How do I join a community?
Go to the Communities page from the sidebar. Browse available communities and click "Join Community" on any that interest you.
How do I edit or delete my profile?
Click the settings (gear) icon in the top navbar or go to your Profile page. You can edit your display name and bio, or delete your profile from the Settings menu.
How do I report a post?
Click the options (≡) button on any post, then select "Report." Fill in the reason and submit. Our moderation team will review it.
Where are my archived posts?
Archived posts are saved in the Archive section. Access it from the sidebar (box icon). You can restore any archived post back to your feed.
How do I create a community?
Go to Communities and click "+ Create Community." Fill in the community name, description, and choose the college/administration. Click Confirm to create it!
